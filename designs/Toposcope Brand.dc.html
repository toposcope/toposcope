§ 01 — the mark One mark: Fault. Source of truth is src/ui/brand-mark.tsx — two filled paths on a 24-unit grid, and nothing else is the logo.
Fault the mark

Two strata, offset along a slip. The product's one job is showing where the line moved — a deploy, a flag, an incident on a time axis — and the mark is that picture with everything else removed. Filled, not drawn: solid bands survive every size with nothing to lose to caps or sub-pixel gaps.

Construction 24 × 24 · slip +4.6, +8.6

One trapezoid, cut twice. Top edge 14.2 units, foot 9.2, depth 5.4 — then the same shape again, slipped +4.6 across, +8.6 down, leaving a 3.2 gap. Every end leans with the slip; the offset between the bands is the whole message. No strokes anywhere in the mark.

§ 03 — colour Six named tokens. Plate is the brand's colour — the mark, every primary button, the favicon's light tile — and a live token in the app (text-plate). Click a swatch to copy it.
The data spectrum is reserved

Level colours mean a level, everywhere, forever. They are not brand colours: never in the mark, never behind a headline, never on a button. The one sanctioned brand use is the five-stripe rule in an eyebrow — a legend, not a decoration.

Service, host and attr splits draw from this palette in source order — other always takes slate and sorts last, whatever slot it drew. Sight doubles as slot two: the accent was already data.

Two whites, and they don't swap

Signal is cool and belongs to type, hairlines and UI. Plate is warm and belongs to the brand: the mark, primary buttons, light surfaces. Body copy never sits in Plate, and no button is ever Signal.
