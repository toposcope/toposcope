Send results

1From a cron line

curl -fsS -m 10 \ https://logs.agental.dev/ping/ 6b1e…a02c/$? No token: the key is in the URL. /start, /fail, /<exit code>; a POST body is the message.

2Any Nagios plugin

toposcope.sh check disk-var -- \ /usr/lib/nagios/plugins/\ check_disk -w 20% -c 10% -p /var Exit 0 ok, 1 warn, 2 fail, 3 unknown; the plugin's first line is the message, its perfdata the value.

3From your own code

POST /v1/checks Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN [{ "monitor": "disk-var", "status": "warn", "value": 87.2, "unit": "%", "every": "5m", "message": "/var 87% used" }] Up to 500 a request. A name not seen before creates its monitor; skip this dialog.
