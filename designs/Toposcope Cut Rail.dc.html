Cut rail vs event detail

frames 2a–2g · follows the locked Cut sheet 1a–1i

The Cut sheet locked the reader onto the results rail — and left one loop undrawn: filter a hex, read a real line, come back to the sets. The answer here is one rule, not a new surface: the rail is a stack. The cut is the base; a selected line pushes its detail on top with a crumb back; the parked cut keeps its state. No second column, no strip tab, no new workspace. The swap from 1b holds — it just gains a floor. Wired in Toposcope App.dc.html.
