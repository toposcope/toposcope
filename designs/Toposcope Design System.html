Motion

Three durations, one curve. Motion is for controls only.

  • Data never moves. Bars, counts and rows repaint in place; live polls keep the last paint.
  • Press scales a button to 0.97 for --d1.
  • Colour and opacity ease over --d2: hover, menus, tooltips, toasts.
  • Position eases over --d3: the tab indicator, a label that changes width.
  • Reduced motion sets all three to 0. Spinners slow down and skeletons stop pulsing.

What the trial changed

  • 1,406 bracketed font sizes to the 7 steps.
  • 154 of 172 inline surface hexes to surface tokens. The rest are text colours or #141416, which the trial missed.
  • 332 bracketed radii to rounded-sm|md|lg|xl.

What it showed

  • Dense screens hold. Hunt changes 9.8% of pixels, mostly 12 → 12.5px text; no row wraps and no column moves.
  • Button's sm was a second default. It was 32px like md; mapping it to 22px left Settings' Save buttons too small, so those callers move to md.
  • 42 Buttons override their own height (h-8 ×15, h-7 ×8, h-[26px] ×8…). Hunt's split Save ▾ kept a 32px chevron beside a 26px Save until its override went. It becomes a Split button.
  • Reading text grows a little. 13.5 → 14 on Settings and Access; the header pages go 13 → 12.5.

# Button  src/ui/components/ui/button.tsx

One action. One primary per surface.

## Props
variant  primary | secondary | outline | ghost | danger     (primary)
size     xs 18 | sm 22 | md 26 | lg 30                      (md)
busy     boolean · sets aria-busy, swallows clicks, keeps focus

## Use
- Toolbars and dialog footers: md. Panel heads: icon (sm 22). In-row: xs.
- A label that changes (Save hunt → Saved) is children; the width eases.

## Do not
- Hand-build a <button> with h-[26px] and rounded-[7px]. Use size.
- Use Button for on/off. That is Switch or Chip.
- Disable while busy. Busy keeps focus; disabled drops it.

## Keys and access
Enter, Space. Icon-only needs aria-label and a Tooltip.

## Motion
Press 0.97 (--duration-1). Reduced motion: none.
