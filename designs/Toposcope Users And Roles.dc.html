Sees only rows matching · the fence service:billing* OR service:checkout Same syntax as the bar and as a share's scope. Added to every search, card, monitor and export this role makes, on the server; they cannot widen it.
Inside the fence Metrics whose labels match service:billing* Traces and profiles of those services Marks for those services, plus every deploy of the platform Rows that arrive through links Fields with the lookup role (client_ip, user_id) shown in full; hashed otherwise

How it looks to them

Home shows only cards whose hunts fall inside the fence. A search for service:api answers "outside your role", not "0 rows", so nobody mistakes a fence for an outage. A share made by this role can never reach past its fence.
